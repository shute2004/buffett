# Third-Party Data Notices

## Worldwide Governance Indicators (World Bank)

`data/countries.csv` contains fields and game-design parameters derived in part from the World Bank's **Worldwide Governance Indicators (WGI)**, including the 2024 political-stability percentile used as an input to some rankings/classifications.

Source:

- World Bank, Worldwide Governance Indicators
- https://datacatalog.worldbank.org/search/dataset/0038026/worldwide-governance-indicators

License:

- Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)
- https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- World Bank dataset terms may include additional terms stated by the World Bank for its datasets.

Changes made in this repository include conversion into board-game-oriented categories, rankings, fixed cash events, roulette outcomes, and other editorial/game-balance transformations. The derived game values should not be interpreted as official World Bank assessments or forecasts.

The first-party source code and original game-design material in this repository remain subject to this repository's `LICENSE`. Third-party data and packages remain subject to their respective licenses and terms.
